    Carbon -- POC, DOC, DIC, POM¶

    NSM1 represents the aquatic carbon cycle with three carbon state variables -- particulate organic carbon (POC), dissolved organic carbon (DOC), and dissolved inorganic carbon (DIC) -- plus a closely related bulk organic matter pool, particulate organic matter (POM). This page documents each state variable, the processes that drive its rate of change, the parameters that govern those processes, and how the carbon pools couple to algae, CBOD, nitrogen, dissolved oxygen, and the atmosphere.

    Role in the Carbon Cycle¶

    Organic carbon enters the water column primarily through algal and benthic algal mortality. Algal death partitions carbon between a particulate pool (POC) and a dissolved pool (DOC) according to the fractions f_pocp and f_pocb. POC is then hydrolyzed to DOC. DOC is mineralized to DIC by heterotrophic bacteria, consuming dissolved oxygen in the process. DIC exchanges with the atmosphere through CO\(_2\) reaeration, is consumed by algal and benthic algal photosynthesis, and is produced by algal respiration, benthic algal respiration, CBOD oxidation, and sediment release.

    POM represents fine detrital organic material suspended in the water column. NSM1 tracks POM separately from POC because POM provides a bulk accounting of non-algal organic solids that settle, dissolve, and contribute to the sediment organic pool. POM is also used when POC is not simulated, in which case POM acts as a surrogate for particulate organic carbon (Zhang and Johnson, 2016).

    flowchart LR
    Ap[Floating Algae] -->|mortality f_pocp| POC
    Ap -->|mortality 1-f_pocp| DOC
    Ap -->|respiration| DIC
    Ap -->|photosynthesis| DIC_sink[DIC uptake]
    Ab[Benthic Algae] -->|mortality f_pocb| POC
    Ab -->|mortality 1-f_pocb| DOC
    Ab -->|respiration| DIC
    Ab -->|photosynthesis| DIC_sink
    Ap -->|settling rda| POM
    Ab -->|mortality 1-Fw| POM
    POC -->|hydrolysis kpoc| DOC
    POC -->|settling vsoc| POC_bed[Bed]
    POC -->|settling to sediment| POM
    POM -->|dissolution kpom| DOC_POM[Dissolved pool]
    POM -->|burial vb| POM_bed[Bed POM]
    DOC -->|oxidation kdoc| DIC
    CBOD -->|oxidation| DIC
    SOD[Sediment] -->|release| DIC
    DIC <-->|CO2 reaeration| Atm[Atmosphere]
    DOC -->|oxidation consumes| DOX

    State Variables¶

    Variable Symbol Units Description
    Particulate organic carbon POC mg-C L\(^{-1}\) Non-living particulate detrital carbon
    Dissolved organic carbon DOC mg-C L\(^{-1}\) Water-column dissolved organic carbon
    Dissolved inorganic carbon DIC mg-C L\(^{-1}\) Sum of H\(_2\)CO\(_3\)*, HCO\(_3^-\), and CO\(_3^{2-}\)
    Particulate organic matter POM mg L\(^{-1}\) Non-algal detrital organic matter (dry mass)

    Units for DIC

    NSM1 stores DIC internally in mg-C L\(^{-1}\). Several DIC flux terms in the source code divide by 12000 to convert mg-C to mol-C when combining with Henry's Law and CO\(_2\) partial pressure, following equation (3.45) of Zhang and Johnson (2016).

    Governing Equations¶

    Particulate Organic Carbon¶

    \[ \frac{d[\text{POC}]}{dt} = F_{pocp}\, k_{dp}(T)\, r_{ca}\, A_p + \frac{1}{h} F_{pocb}\, k_{db}(T)\, r_{cb}\, A_b\, F_b\, F_w - k_{poc}(T)\, [\text{POC}] - \frac{v_{soc}}{h}\, [\text{POC}] \]

    The first two terms are algal and benthic algal mortality. The third term is first-order POC hydrolysis to DOC. The fourth term is gravitational settling.

    Dissolved Organic Carbon¶

    \[ \frac{d[\text{DOC}]}{dt} = (1 - F_{pocp})\, k_{dp}(T)\, r_{ca}\, A_p + \frac{1}{h}(1 - F_{pocb})\, k_{db}(T)\, r_{cb}\, A_b\, F_b\, F_w + k_{poc}(T)\, [\text{POC}] - \frac{[\text{DOX}]}{K_{sOxmc} + [\text{DOX}]}\, k_{doc}(T)\, [\text{DOC}] \]

    DOC gains carbon from the dissolved fraction of algal and benthic algal mortality and from POC hydrolysis. DOC is lost by first-order oxidation to DIC, attenuated by a Monod oxygen-limitation term.

    Potential porting discrepancy

    The current dDOCdt implementation in processes.py (~line 2651) omits two terms present in Zhang and Johnson (2016), Eq. 3.44: a POM dissolution source, \(+ k_{pom}(T)\, f_{com}\, [\text{POM}]\), and a denitrification sink proportional to kdnit and NO3. As implemented, DOC does not receive carbon from dissolving POM and is not consumed by denitrification. This is a potential porting discrepancy worth review, since the kinetic formulations should conform to the authoritative report. Confirm before calibrating DOC against measurements.

    Dissolved Inorganic Carbon¶

    \[ \frac{d[\text{DIC}]}{dt} = \text{Atm}_{CO_2} + \frac{k_{rp}(T)\, r_{ca}\, A_p}{12000} - \frac{\mu_p\, r_{ca}\, A_p}{12000} + \frac{k_{rb}(T)\, r_{cb}\, A_b\, F_b}{12000\, h} - \frac{\mu_b\, r_{cb}\, A_b\, F_b}{12000\, h} + \text{DIC}_{CBOD} + \frac{\text{SOD}(T)}{12000\, r_{oc}\, h} \]

    DIC receives carbon from atmospheric CO\(_2\) reaeration, algal and benthic algal respiration, CBOD oxidation, and sediment release. DIC is consumed by algal and benthic algal photosynthesis. The divisor 12000 converts mg-C L\(^{-1}\) to mol-C L\(^{-1}\) for terms that couple to Henry's Law.

    Particulate Organic Matter¶

    \[ \frac{d[\text{POM}]}{dt} = \frac{v_{sap}\, r_{da}\, A_p}{h_2} + \frac{v_{soc}\, [\text{POC}]}{h_2\, f_{com}} + \frac{k_{db}(T)\, A_b\, F_b\, (1 - F_w)}{h_2} - k_{pom}(T)\, [\text{POM}] - \frac{v_b\, [\text{POM}]}{h_2} \]

    POM gains mass from algal settling, settling of POC into the sediment layer (converted to dry mass by fcom), and benthic algal mortality that is not routed to the water column. POM is lost by dissolution (first-order, returning material to the dissolved pool) and by burial into deeper sediments.

    Potential porting discrepancy: POM pool identity

    The current dPOMdt implementation in processes.py (~line 2297) uses the active sediment layer thickness h2 as its denominator and includes a burial sink POM_burial. This matches the sediment POM balance (Zhang and Johnson, 2016, Eq. 3.38) rather than the water-column POM balance (Eq. 3.37). Since NSM1 carries POM as a water-column state variable in the overview, this is a potential porting discrepancy worth review. Treat POM as the sediment-layer particulate organic matter concentration unless a configuration revision clarifies the intended pool.

    Process Equations¶

    POM Hydrolysis and Dissolution¶

    The POM dissolution rate at water temperature follows the Arrhenius correction \(k_{pom}(T) = k_{pom,20}\, \theta^{T-20}\). Dissolution returns particulate organic matter to dissolved forms and is the primary water-column loss term for POM other than burial.

    POC Hydrolysis¶

    POC hydrolysis is a first-order decay that converts particulate organic carbon to dissolved organic carbon:

    \[ \text{POC hydrolysis} = k_{poc}(T)\, [\text{POC}] \]

    The POC_hydrolysis function in processes.py (~line 2455) does not include an oxygen attenuation term; hydrolysis proceeds at the temperature-corrected rate regardless of DOX, consistent with Zhang and Johnson (2016), Eq. 3.42.

    DOC Mineralization¶

    DOC oxidation to DIC is attenuated by dissolved oxygen through a Monod term:

    \[ \text{DOC oxidation} = \frac{[\text{DOX}]}{K_{sOxmc} + [\text{DOX}]}\, k_{doc}(T)\, [\text{DOC}] \]

    When use_DOX = False, the oxygen attenuation is dropped and oxidation proceeds at \(k_{doc}(T)\, [\text{DOC}]\). DOC oxidation is both a DOC sink and a DOX sink; the corresponding DOX consumption is computed in the dissolved oxygen module using the carbon-to-oxygen ratio roc.

    Algal Photosynthesis and Respiration (DIC)¶

    Photosynthesis removes DIC in proportion to algal growth; respiration returns DIC to the water column:

    \[ \text{DIC}_{\text{photosynthesis}} = \frac{\mu_p\, r_{ca}\, A_p}{12000}, \qquad \text{DIC}_{\text{respiration}} = \frac{k_{rp}(T)\, r_{ca}\, A_p}{12000} \]

    Benthic algal contributions use the benthic carbon-to-dry-mass ratio rcb and are normalized by depth h.

    CO\(_2\) Reaeration¶

    The model couples DIC to the atmosphere through a Henry's Law driven flux:

    \[ \text{Atm}_{CO_2} = 0.923\, k_a(T)\, \left( \frac{K_H\, p_{CO_2}}{10^6} - F_{CO_2}\, [\text{DIC}] \right) \]

    The factor 0.923 approximates \((MW_{O_2}/MW_{CO_2})^{0.25} = (32/44)^{0.25}\) and converts the oxygen reaeration rate to a CO\(_2\) reaeration rate, following equation (3.47) of Zhang and Johnson (2016). K_H is computed from the temperature-dependent formulation of Edmond and Gieskes (1970).

    Parameters¶

    Carbon parameters (DEFAULT_CARBON)¶

    Parameter Units Default Description
    f_pocp unitless 0.9 Fraction of floating algal mortality routed to POC
    f_pocb unitless 0.9 Fraction of benthic algal mortality routed to POC
    kpoc_20 d\(^{-1}\) 0.005 POC hydrolysis rate at 20 C
    kpoc_theta unitless 1.047 Arrhenius coefficient for POC hydrolysis
    kdoc_20 d\(^{-1}\) 0.01 DOC oxidation rate at 20 C
    kdoc_theta unitless 1.047 Arrhenius coefficient for DOC oxidation
    KsOxmc mg-O\(_2\) L\(^{-1}\) 1.0 Half-saturation oxygen attenuation for DOC oxidation
    pCO2 ppm 383.0 Atmospheric CO\(_2\) partial pressure
    FCO2 unitless 0.2 Fraction of DIC present as CO\(_2\)
    roc mg-O\(_2\) mg-C\(^{-1}\) 32/12 O\(_2\) to C ratio for carbon oxidation
    { .parameter-table }

    Parameter Units Default Description

    Sentinel defaults

    Several global defaults in DEFAULT_GLOBALVARS use sentinel placeholders (for example, vsop, vs, SOD_20, and kaw_20_user are set to 999) and must be overridden before running the model. See the NSM1 overview for details.

    Couplings with Other State Variables¶

    • Algae and benthic algae supply the primary source of POC and DOC via mortality and consume DIC through photosynthesis. Algal growth and respiration rates (ApGrowth, ApRespiration, AbGrowth, AbRespiration) are computed in the algae module and passed to the DIC flux terms.
    • Dissolved oxygen attenuates DOC oxidation through KsOxmc and is consumed by DOC and CBOD oxidation (through roc) and by algal respiration.
    • CBOD contributes to DIC through DIC_CBOD_oxidation, which uses kbod_tc and KsOxbod from the CBOD group. See the CBOD page.
    • Sediment oxygen demand releases DIC to the water column at rate \(\text{SOD}(T) / (12000\, r_{oc}\, h)\).
    • POM exchanges mass with POC (through settling in V1) and, in the Zhang and Johnson (2016) formulation, with DOC via dissolution.

    Configuration¶

    Pre-release configuration format

    The NSM1 YAML configuration schema is under active development. The snippet below illustrates the intended structure. Field names may change before the first tagged release.

    nsm1:
  carbon:
    f_pocp: 0.9
    f_pocb: 0.9
    kpoc_20: 0.005
    kpoc_theta: 1.047
    kdoc_20: 0.01
    kdoc_theta: 1.047
    KsOxmc: 1.0
    pCO2: 383.0
    FCO2: 0.2
    roc: 2.6667
  pom:
    kpom_20: 0.1
    kpom_theta: 1.047
    h2: 0.1
  globals:
    vsoc: 0.25
    vb: 0.001
    fcom: 0.4
  flags:
    use_POC: true
    use_DOC: true
    use_DIC: true
    use_POM: true

    Implementation Notes¶

    • NSM1 process functions are defined in src/clearwater_modules/nsm1/processes.py:
      • POM: dPOMdt at ~line 2297, supported by POM_algal_settling, POM_dissolution, POM_POC_settling, POM_benthic_algae_mortality, POM_burial, and kpom_tc.
      • POC: dPOCdt at ~line 2532, supported by POC_hydrolysis, POC_settling, POC_algal_mortality, and POC_benthic_algae_mortality.
      • DOC: dDOCdt at ~line 2651, supported by DOC_algal_mortality, DOC_benthic_algae_mortality, DOC_DIC_oxidation, and kdoc_tc.
      • DIC: dDICdt at ~line 2834, supported by Atmospheric_CO2_reaeration, DIC_algal_respiration, DIC_algal_photosynthesis, DIC_benthic_algae_respiration, DIC_benthic_algae_photosynthesis, DIC_CBOD_oxidation, and DIC_sed_release.
    • Default parameters are defined in src/clearwater_modules/nsm1/constants.py as DEFAULT_CARBON and DEFAULT_POM.
    • V2 implementation pending. Carbon, CBOD, and POM have not yet been ported to the v2 process registration pattern. See the Adding a Process developer guide for the expected port structure.
