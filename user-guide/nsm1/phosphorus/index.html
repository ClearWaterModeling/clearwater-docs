    Phosphorus Cycle -- OrgP, TIP¶

    NSM1 represents aquatic phosphorus cycling through two coupled state variables: organic phosphorus (OrgP) and total inorganic phosphorus (TIP). Together these variables track the flow of phosphorus from detrital organic matter through mineralization, algal assimilation, settling, sorption, and sediment-water exchange.

    Role in the Nutrient Cycle¶

    The NSM1 phosphorus cycle follows the progression OrgP -> TIP -> algal uptake with parallel settling and bed-release pathways. Algal and benthic algal mortality release phosphorus into the organic pool. First-order mineralization hydrolyzes OrgP to the dissolved inorganic fraction of TIP. Floating and benthic algae assimilate dissolved inorganic phosphorus (DIP) during growth and return it during respiration. A portion of TIP sorbs to suspended solids and settles to the bed; the remaining dissolved fraction is available for biological uptake. Sediment release of DIP returns phosphorus to the water column.

    flowchart LR
    Algae[Floating Algae Ap] -->|death| OrgP
    BAlgae[Benthic Algae Ab] -->|death| OrgP
    OrgP -->|mineralization| TIP
    OrgP -->|settling| Bed1[Bed]
    TIP -->|uptake| Algae
    TIP -->|uptake| BAlgae
    Algae -->|respiration| TIP
    BAlgae -->|respiration| TIP
    TIP -->|sorbed-fraction settling| Bed2[Bed]
    Bed3[Bed] -->|diffusion| TIP

    State Variables¶

    Symbol Units Description
    OrgP mg-P L\(^{-1}\) Dissolved and particulate organic phosphorus
    TIP mg-P L\(^{-1}\) Total inorganic phosphorus (dissolved plus sorbed)

    NSM1 also computes several diagnostic totals: DIP = fdp * TIP (dissolved inorganic P), TOP = OrgP + rpa * Ap (total organic P including algal P), and TP = TOP + TIP.

    Governing Equations¶

    The organic phosphorus budget accounts for algal death inputs, mineralization loss, and settling:

    \[ \frac{d\,\mathrm{OrgP}}{dt} = r_{pa}\,k_{dp}\,A_p \;+\; \frac{r_{pb}\,F_w\,F_b\,k_{db}\,A_b}{h} \;-\; k_{op}(T)\,\mathrm{OrgP} \;-\; \frac{v_{sop}}{h}\,\mathrm{OrgP} \]

    The total inorganic phosphorus budget gains mass from mineralization, algal respiration, and bed release, and loses mass through settling of the sorbed fraction and algal uptake:

    \[ \frac{d\,\mathrm{TIP}}{dt} = k_{op}(T)\,\mathrm{OrgP} \;-\; \frac{v_s}{h}(1 - f_{dp})\,\mathrm{TIP} \;+\; \frac{r_{PO4}(T)}{h} \;+\; r_{pa}\,k_{rp}\,A_p \;-\; r_{pa}\,\mu_p\,A_p \;+\; \frac{r_{pb}\,F_b\,k_{rb}\,A_b}{h} \;-\; \frac{r_{pb}\,F_b\,\mu_b\,A_b}{h} \]

    In these expressions \(h\) is water depth, \(T\) is water temperature, rate constants suffixed with \((T)\) are Arrhenius-corrected, and \(f_{dp}\) is the dissolved fraction of inorganic phosphorus controlled by partitioning to suspended solids.

    Process Equations¶

    Mineralization (OrgP -> TIP)¶

    Organic phosphorus is hydrolyzed to dissolved inorganic phosphorus at a first-order, temperature-corrected rate:

    \[ \mathrm{OrgP\_DIP\_decay} = k_{op}(T)\,\mathrm{OrgP} \]

    The rate constant is kop_20 with Arrhenius coefficient kop_theta. This flux appears as a sink in dOrgPdt and a source in dTIPdt.

    Organic Phosphorus Settling¶

    Particulate organic phosphorus settles at velocity vsop:

    \[ \mathrm{OrgP\_Settling} = \frac{v_{sop}}{h}\,\mathrm{OrgP} \]

    Settling-velocity calibration

    The default vsop is 0.1 m d\(^{-1}\) and the default suspended-solids settling velocity vs is 0.1 m d\(^{-1}\). Both are within the typical range of 0.01--1.0 m d\(^{-1}\) for organic phosphorus and suspended solids in lowland streams; calibrate against site-specific measurements where available.

    TIP Partitioning and Settling¶

    NSM1 represents inorganic phosphorus partitioning between dissolved (DIP) and sorbed (particulate) phases through the dissolved fraction fdp:

    \[ f_{dp} = \frac{1}{1 + K_{dpo4}\,\mathrm{Solid}}, \qquad \mathrm{DIP} = f_{dp}\,\mathrm{TIP} \]

    The sorbed fraction (1 - fdp) * TIP settles with the suspended solids at velocity vs:

    \[ \mathrm{TIP\_Settling} = \frac{v_s}{h}\,(1 - f_{dp})\,\mathrm{TIP} \]

    The partition coefficient kdpo4 and the solids concentration Solid together determine the sorbed fraction.

    The dissolved fraction fdp is computed internally by the Phosphorus process from kdpo4 and the suspended-solids concentration Solid at each time step, following Zhang and Johnson (2016), Eq. 3.27. There is no need to supply fdp from outside the module; the partition coefficient kdpo4 and the solids concentration are the user-facing parameters.

    Bed Release of TIP¶

    The sediment bed supplies dissolved inorganic phosphorus to the overlying water at a temperature-corrected areal flux rpo4_tc, distributed over the water depth:

    \[ \mathrm{DIPfromBed} = \frac{r_{PO4}(T)}{h} \]

    The default rpo4_20 in DEFAULT_PHOSPHORUS is 0, so users must override this value to activate benthic phosphorus flux when the sediment flux module is disabled.

    Algal Uptake (TIP -> Algae)¶

    Floating and benthic algae consume dissolved inorganic phosphorus in proportion to their growth rate and phosphorus-to-biomass stoichiometric ratios:

    \[ \mathrm{DIP\_ApGrowth} = r_{pa}\,\mu_p\,A_p, \qquad \mathrm{DIP\_AbGrowth} = \frac{r_{pb}\,F_b\,\mu_b\,A_b}{h} \]

    NSM1 does not partition phosphorus uptake between dissolved and sorbed pools; all phosphorus uptake is drawn from TIP.

    Algal Respiration and Death¶

    Algal respiration returns phosphorus to TIP; algal death transfers phosphorus to OrgP:

    \[ \mathrm{DIP\_ApRespiration} = r_{pa}\,k_{rp}\,A_p, \qquad \mathrm{ApDeath\_OrgP} = r_{pa}\,k_{dp}\,A_p \]

    Benthic algal contributions include the water-column death fraction Fw, the benthic area fraction Fb, and the depth normalization 1/h.

    Parameters¶

    Defaults for the phosphorus-specific rates are taken from DEFAULT_PHOSPHORUS in src/clearwater_modules/nsm1/constants.py. Settling velocities are taken from DEFAULT_GLOBALVARS.

    Parameter Units Scope Default Description
    kop_20 d\(^{-1}\) global 0.1 OrgP mineralization rate constant at 20 C
    kop_theta -- global 1.047 Arrhenius coefficient for OrgP mineralization
    rpo4_20 g-P m\(^{-2}\) d\(^{-1}\) global 0 Bed release rate of DIP at 20 C
    rpo4_theta -- global 1.074 Arrhenius coefficient for DIP bed release
    kdpo4 L mg\(^{-1}\) global 0.0 Partition coefficient of inorganic P to suspended solids
    vsop m d\(^{-1}\) global 0.1 Organic phosphorus settling velocity
    vs m d\(^{-1}\) global 0.1 Suspended solids settling velocity
    Solid -- global 1 Suspended solids concentration index used in partitioning
    { .parameter-table }

    Stoichiometric ratios that couple phosphorus to the algal modules:

    Parameter Units Source Default Description
    AWp mg-P mg-A\(^{-1}\) DEFAULT_ALGAE 1 Algal phosphorus-to-dry-weight stoichiometric ratio
    BWp mg-P mg-D\(^{-1}\) DEFAULT_BALGAE 1 Benthic algal P-to-dry-weight stoichiometric ratio
    rpa mg-P ug-Chla\(^{-1}\) derived -- AWp / AWa, phosphorus-to-chlorophyll ratio
    rpb mg-P mg-D\(^{-1}\) derived -- Benthic algal phosphorus-to-biomass ratio
    { .parameter-table }

    Couplings with Other State Variables¶

    • Floating algae (Ap): Algal growth withdraws TIP; respiration returns TIP; death supplies OrgP. Couplings use the stoichiometric ratio rpa.
    • Benthic algae (Ab): Same couplings as floating algae, scaled by Fb, Fw, and depth, using rpb.
    • Suspended solids: The partition coefficient kdpo4 controls the sorbed fraction of TIP, which settles at velocity vs. Settled phosphorus is removed from the water column and contributes to bed phosphorus inventory.
    • Alkalinity (Alk): NSM1 phosphorus processes do not directly alter alkalinity, but algal growth couples nitrogen and phosphorus uptake through shared growth rates.
    • Dissolved oxygen (DOX): OrgP mineralization does not explicitly consume DOX in NSM1; oxygen consumption associated with organic matter decomposition is captured through CBOD and DOC oxidation.

    Configuration¶

    phosphorus:
  use_OrgP: true
  use_TIP: true

  # Rate constants at 20 C
  kop_20: 0.1
  rpo4_20: 0.05    # set positive value to enable sediment DIP release

  # Arrhenius coefficients
  kop_theta: 1.047
  rpo4_theta: 1.074

  # Partitioning
  kdpo4: 0.0       # set >0 to enable P sorption to solids

  # Settling velocities
  vsop: 0.1
  vs: 0.1

    Implementation Notes¶

    • Source: src/clearwater_modules/processes/phosphorus.py. The Phosphorus process class implements the OrgP and TIP rate functions and the fdp partitioning helper, with temperature-correction helpers kop_tc and rpo4_tc.
    • Temperature correction uses the standard Arrhenius form rc20 * theta^(T - 20) through clearwater_modules.kinetics.arrhenius_correction.
    • Diagnostic totals exposed by the process: DIP = fdp * TIP, TOP = OrgP + rpa * Ap, and TP = TOP + TIP.

    Benthic release consistency

    Zhang and Johnson (2016), Table 6 (pp. 62--66), lists rpo4 as a benthic sediment release rate in g-P m\(^{-2}\) d\(^{-1}\). NSM1 applies the Arrhenius correction directly to rpo4_20 and divides by depth, which is dimensionally consistent with an areal flux, matching the report. Users configuring rpo4_20 should specify the value in g-P m\(^{-2}\) d\(^{-1}\).
