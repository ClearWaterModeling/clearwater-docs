    Alkalinity (Alk)¶

    Alkalinity is the acid-neutralizing capacity of water, operationally defined as the sum of titratable bases. In surface waters dominated by the carbonate system, alkalinity is well approximated by:

    \[ Alk \approx [HCO_3^-] + 2[CO_3^{2-}] + [OH^-] - [H^+] \]

    NSM1 tracks total alkalinity Alk as a prognostic state variable in units of mg-CaCO3 L-1. Alkalinity determines the pH buffering capacity of the water column, sets the equilibrium distribution of dissolved inorganic carbon among CO2, bicarbonate, and carbonate species, and controls the saturation state of calcium carbonate minerals. Biological processes that produce or consume hydrogen ions (H+) shift alkalinity, and NSM1 accounts for the dominant biological drivers explicitly.

    Role in Water Quality¶

    Alkalinity in NSM1 is driven entirely by biologically mediated transformations of nitrogen and by autotrophic growth and respiration. The model includes six flux terms:

    • Nitrification of NH4 to NO3 releases H+ and decreases alkalinity.
    • Denitrification of NO3 to N2 consumes H+ and increases alkalinity.
    • Algal growth on NH4 consumes H+ and increases alkalinity; algal growth on NO3 releases H+ and decreases alkalinity.
    • Algal respiration releases NH4, consuming H+ and increasing alkalinity.
    • Benthic algal growth and respiration contribute the same way, scaled by the benthic coverage fraction and depth.

    NSM1 does not explicitly simulate CO\(_2\) reaeration effects on alkalinity (CO\(_2\) exchange affects DIC but is alkalinity-neutral) or mineral dissolution and precipitation.

    Governing Equation¶

    The Alk mass balance, implemented in dAlkdt at processes.py ~line 3413, combines the six biologically driven terms:

    \[ \frac{d[Alk]}{dt} = F_{denit} - F_{nit} - F_{ApG} + F_{ApR} - F_{AbG} + F_{AbR} \]

    Each flux is expressed in mg-CaCO3 L-1 d-1. The factor 50000 (equivalent weight of CaCO3 in mg eq-1) appears in every term to convert from molar equivalents to mg-CaCO3.

    Process Equations¶

    Alkalinity change from nitrification¶

    Nitrification of 1 mole of NH4 releases 2 moles of H+, decreasing alkalinity. The Alk_nitrification routine at ~line 3284 computes:

    \[ F_{nit} = r_{alkn} \left(1 - e^{-KNR \cdot DOX}\right) k_{nit}(T) \cdot NH4 \cdot 50000 \]

    The DOX inhibition term \((1 - e^{-KNR \cdot DOX})\) matches the oxygen dependence of the nitrification rate in the nitrogen and DOX modules. When use_DOX is false, NSM1 drops the inhibition factor.

    Alkalinity change from denitrification¶

    Denitrification of NO3 to N2 consumes H+, increasing alkalinity. The Alk_denitrification routine at ~line 3246 computes:

    \[ F_{denit} = r_{alkden} \left(1 - \frac{DOX}{DOX + K_{sOxdn}}\right) k_{dnit}(T) \cdot NO3 \cdot 50000 \]

    The DOX inhibition term promotes denitrification only at low oxygen. When use_DOX is false, the oxygen term drops out.

    Alkalinity change from algal growth¶

    Algal growth on ammonium consumes H+ (increases alkalinity); growth on nitrate releases H+ (decreases alkalinity). The Alk_algal_growth routine at ~line 3322 computes the net effect weighted by the ammonium preference fraction \(f_{NH4}^{Ap}\):

    \[ F_{ApG} = \left[ r_{alkaa} \, f_{NH4}^{Ap} - r_{alkan} \, (1 - f_{NH4}^{Ap}) \right] \cdot ApGrowth \cdot r_{ca} \cdot 50000 \]

    The sign convention in the governing equation subtracts \(F_{ApG}\), reflecting that growth on NO3 is the net alkalinity-consuming pathway when the ammonium fraction is low.

    Alkalinity change from algal respiration¶

    Algal respiration returns NH4 to the water, which consumes H+ and increases alkalinity. The Alk_algal_respiration routine at ~line 3345 computes:

    \[ F_{ApR} = ApResp \cdot r_{alkaa} \cdot r_{ca} \cdot 50000 \]

    Alkalinity change from benthic algae¶

    Benthic algal growth and respiration follow the same stoichiometry as floating algae, with the benthic ratios r_alkba and r_alkbn, the benthic C-to-dry-weight ratio rcb, and additional scaling by the benthic coverage fraction Fb and inverse depth \(1/h\). The Alk_benthic_algae_growth (~line 3364) and Alk_benthic_algae_respiration (~line 3391) routines compute:

    \[ F_{AbG} = \frac{F_b}{h} \left[ r_{alkba} \, f_{NH4}^{Ab} - r_{alkbn} \, (1 - f_{NH4}^{Ab}) \right] \cdot AbGrowth \cdot r_{cb} \cdot 50000 \]
    \[ F_{AbR} = \frac{F_b}{h} \cdot r_{alkba} \cdot AbResp \cdot r_{cb} \cdot 50000 \]

    Parameters¶

    Parameter Units Scope Description Default
    r_alkaa eq / ug-Chla alkalinity Alkalinity change per unit algal growth when NH4 is the N source 14/106/12/1000
    r_alkan eq / ug-Chla alkalinity Alkalinity change per unit algal growth when NO3 is the N source 18/106/12/1000
    r_alkn eq / mg-N alkalinity Alkalinity change per unit NH4 nitrified 2/14/1000
    r_alkden eq / mg-N alkalinity Alkalinity change per unit NO3 denitrified 4/14/1000
    r_alkba eq / mg-D alkalinity Alkalinity change per unit benthic algal growth when NH4 is the N source 14/106/12/1000
    r_alkbn eq / mg-D alkalinity Alkalinity change per unit benthic algal growth when NO3 is the N source 18/106/12/1000
    KNR mg-O2 L-1 nitrogen Oxygen inhibition factor for nitrification 0.6
    KsOxdn mg-O2 L-1 nitrogen Half-saturation oxygen inhibition for denitrification 0.1
    { .parameter-table }

    The Redfield-derived ratios (14/106/12/1000 and 18/106/12/1000) encode the number of moles of H+ produced or consumed per mole of algal C fixed through the Redfield stoichiometry (C106 H263 O110 N16 P), divided by the C:Chl conversion and unit factors. Each ratio is multiplied by 50000 mg-CaCO3 eq-1 inside the flux functions to produce mg-CaCO3 L-1 d-1.

    Couplings with Other State Variables¶

    Alkalinity is a passive tracer in NSM1: it is affected by other state variables but does not feed back into them. Its couplings are:

    • Ammonia (NH4) drives nitrification loss of alkalinity and, through algal uptake, controls the sign of the algal growth term.
    • Nitrate (NO3) drives denitrification gain of alkalinity and contributes to the algal growth term when algae use NO3.
    • Floating algae (Ap) drives \(F_{ApG}\) and \(F_{ApR}\).
    • Benthic algae (Ab) drives \(F_{AbG}\) and \(F_{AbR}\).
    • Dissolved oxygen (DOX) modulates the nitrification and denitrification rates through their oxygen dependence.
    • Depth (h) scales the benthic algae contributions.

    Configuration¶

    global_parameters:
  use_Alk: true
  use_NH4: true
  use_NO3: true
  use_DOX: true
  use_Algae: true
  use_Balgae: true

alkalinity_parameters:
  r_alkaa: 1.100629e-05   # 14 / 106 / 12 / 1000
  r_alkan: 1.415094e-05   # 18 / 106 / 12 / 1000
  r_alkn: 1.428571e-04    # 2 / 14 / 1000
  r_alkden: 2.857143e-04  # 4 / 14 / 1000
  r_alkba: 1.100629e-05
  r_alkbn: 1.415094e-05

    Unit conventions

    The alkalinity stoichiometric ratios are stored as pure equivalence ratios (mol eq per unit biomass or nitrogen). Each flux function multiplies by 50000 (the equivalent weight of CaCO3 in mg per mol eq) to produce the final flux in mg-CaCO3 L-1 d-1. Do not pre-multiply the ratios by 50000 in the configuration file.

    Implementation Notes¶

    • NSM1 source: src/clearwater_modules/nsm1/processes.py, function dAlkdt at ~line 3413. Supporting routines Alk_denitrification (~line 3246), Alk_nitrification (~line 3284), Alk_algal_growth (~line 3322), Alk_algal_respiration (~line 3345), Alk_benthic_algae_growth (~line 3364), and Alk_benthic_algae_respiration (~line 3391) compute the individual terms.
    • v2 implementation: the alkalinity process is not yet available in the v2 process-based architecture.
    • Default constants: DEFAULT_ALKALINITY in src/clearwater_modules/nsm1/constants.py.

    Potential porting discrepancy

    In the Alk_nitrification function, the second branch of the np.select (used when use_NH4 is true but use_DOX is false) omits the r_alkn factor, returning knit_tc * NH4 * 50000 instead of r_alkn * knit_tc * NH4 * 50000. Zhang and Johnson (2016), Section 3.12, applies r_alkn in all branches, and the DOX-enabled branch in the current code does the same. This is a potential porting discrepancy worth review, since the kinetic formulation should conform to the authoritative report. Users running NSM1 without the DOX module should verify the computed nitrification-driven alkalinity flux against expected values, or enable use_DOX to ensure the stoichiometric ratio is applied.
