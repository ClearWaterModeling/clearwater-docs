    Nitrogen Cycle -- OrgN, NH4, NO3, N2¶

    NSM1 represents the aquatic nitrogen cycle through four coupled state variables: organic nitrogen (OrgN), ammonia nitrogen (NH4), nitrate nitrogen (NO3), and dinitrogen gas (N2). Together these variables track the flow of nitrogen from detrital organic matter through mineralization, nitrification, denitrification, and algal assimilation.

    Role in the Nutrient Cycle¶

    The NSM1 nitrogen cycle follows the canonical progression OrgN -> NH4 -> NO3 -> N2. Algal and benthic algal mortality release nitrogen to the OrgN pool. Heterotrophic bacteria hydrolyze OrgN to NH4 (mineralization). Nitrifying bacteria oxidize NH4 to NO3 (nitrification), consuming dissolved oxygen. Under low-oxygen conditions, denitrifiers reduce NO3 to dinitrogen gas (N2), which provides an anaerobic nitrogen sink to the atmosphere. Floating and benthic algae assimilate both NH4 and NO3 for growth, with preference controlled by the ammonia preference factor. Bed release supplies additional NH4, and sediment denitrification removes NO3.

    flowchart LR
    Algae[Floating Algae Ap] -->|death| OrgN
    BAlgae[Benthic Algae Ab] -->|death| OrgN
    OrgN -->|mineralization| NH4
    OrgN -->|settling| Bed1[Bed]
    Bed2[Bed] -->|diffusion| NH4
    NH4 -->|nitrification| NO3
    NH4 -->|uptake| Algae
    NH4 -->|uptake| BAlgae
    NO3 -->|uptake| Algae
    NO3 -->|uptake| BAlgae
    NO3 -->|water-column denitrification| N2
    NO3 -->|sediment denitrification| N2
    Algae -->|respiration| NH4
    BAlgae -->|respiration| NH4
    N2 -->|degassing| Atm[Atmosphere]

    State Variables¶

    Symbol Units Description
    OrgN mg-N L\(^{-1}\) Dissolved and particulate organic nitrogen
    NH4 mg-N L\(^{-1}\) Ammonium plus ammonia nitrogen
    NO3 mg-N L\(^{-1}\) Nitrate plus nitrite nitrogen
    N2 mg-N L\(^{-1}\) Dinitrogen gas (tracked when use_N2 = True)

    Governing Equations¶

    The rate of change of each nitrogen state variable is the net balance of its source and sink terms. The organic nitrogen budget accounts for algal death inputs, mineralization loss, and settling to the bed:

    \[ \frac{d\,\mathrm{OrgN}}{dt} = r_{na}\,k_{dp}\,A_p \;+\; \frac{r_{nb}\,F_w\,F_b\,k_{db}\,A_b}{h} \;-\; k_{on}(T)\,\mathrm{OrgN} \;-\; \frac{v_{son}}{h}\,\mathrm{OrgN} \]

    The ammonium budget sums mineralization inflow, bed release, algal respiration inflow, and subtracts nitrification and algal assimilation:

    \[ \frac{d\,\mathrm{NH4}}{dt} = k_{on}(T)\,\mathrm{OrgN} \;-\; f_{NR}\,k_{nit}(T)\,\mathrm{NH4} \;+\; \frac{r_{NH4}(T)}{h} \;+\; r_{na}\,k_{rp}\,A_p \;-\; P_N\,r_{na}\,\mu_p\,A_p \;+\; \frac{r_{nb}\,F_b\,k_{rb}\,A_b}{h} \;-\; \frac{P_{Nb}\,r_{nb}\,F_b\,\mu_b\,A_b}{h} \]

    The nitrate budget gains nitrogen from nitrification and loses it to water-column denitrification, sediment denitrification, and algal uptake of the nitrate fraction:

    \[ \frac{d\,\mathrm{NO3}}{dt} = f_{NR}\,k_{nit}(T)\,\mathrm{NH4} \;-\; \left(1 - \frac{\mathrm{DOX}}{\mathrm{DOX} + K_{sOxdn}}\right) k_{dnit}(T)\,\mathrm{NO3} \;-\; \frac{v_{NO3}(T)}{h}\,\mathrm{NO3} \;-\; (1-P_N)\,r_{na}\,\mu_p\,A_p \;-\; \frac{(1-P_{Nb})\,r_{nb}\,F_b\,\mu_b\,A_b}{h} \]

    The dinitrogen budget receives mass from water-column and sediment denitrification and loses mass through gas transfer to the atmosphere.

    In these expressions \(h\) is water depth, \(T\) is water temperature, and rate constants suffixed with \((T)\) are Arrhenius-corrected as \(k_{20}\,\theta^{T-20}\).

    Process Equations¶

    Mineralization (OrgN -> NH4)¶

    Organic nitrogen is hydrolyzed to ammonium at a first-order, temperature-corrected rate:

    \[ \mathrm{OrgN\_NH4\_Decay} = k_{on}(T)\,\mathrm{OrgN} \]

    The temperature correction uses the kon_20 rate constant and kon_theta Arrhenius coefficient. This flux appears as a sink in the dOrgNdt equation and as a source in dNH4dt.

    Organic Nitrogen Settling¶

    Particulate organic nitrogen settles from the water column to the bed at velocity vson:

    \[ \mathrm{OrgN\_Settling} = \frac{v_{son}}{h}\,\mathrm{OrgN} \]

    Nitrification (NH4 -> NO3)¶

    Autotrophic nitrification oxidizes ammonium to nitrate. The model includes a low-oxygen inhibition factor NitrificationInhibition that multiplies the first-order nitrification rate:

    \[ f_{NR} = 1 - e^{-K_{NR}\,\mathrm{DOX}} \]
    \[ \mathrm{NH4\_Nitrification} = f_{NR}\,k_{nit}(T)\,\mathrm{NH4} \]

    When use_DOX is false, the inhibition factor is set to 1.0. The nitrification rate constant is knit_20 with Arrhenius coefficient knit_theta. The inhibition half-coefficient KNR controls how sharply nitrification shuts down as DOX approaches zero.

    Inhibition form

    Zhang and Johnson (2016) present the nitrification inhibition term as an exponential of the form \(1 - e^{-K_{NR}\,\mathrm{DOX}}\). The NitrificationInhibition function in processes.py (~line 1422) implements this exact form, consistent with the report.

    Bed Release of NH4¶

    The sediment bed supplies ammonium to the overlying water at a temperature-corrected areal flux rnh4_tc, distributed over the water depth:

    \[ \mathrm{NH4fromBed} = \frac{r_{NH4}(T)}{h} \]

    The rate rnh4_20 has default 0 in DEFAULT_NITROGEN, so users must override this value to enable benthic ammonium flux when the sediment flux module is disabled.

    Denitrification (NO3 -> N2)¶

    Water-column denitrification proceeds only under oxygen-limited conditions. The model applies a Monod-type oxygen inhibition:

    \[ \mathrm{NO3\_Denit} = \left(1 - \frac{\mathrm{DOX}}{\mathrm{DOX} + K_{sOxdn}}\right) k_{dnit}(T)\,\mathrm{NO3} \]

    The denitrification rate constant is kdnit_20 with Arrhenius coefficient kdnit_theta. The half-saturation oxygen inhibition constant KsOxdn determines the oxygen level at which denitrification reaches half of its anoxic maximum.

    Sediment denitrification represents NO3 diffusing into the anoxic sediment bed where it is reduced to N2:

    \[ \mathrm{NO3\_BedDenit} = \frac{v_{NO3}(T)}{h}\,\mathrm{NO3} \]

    The mass transfer velocity vno3_20 (default 0) must be set to a positive value for this flux to be active.

    N2 prognostic budget

    N2 is a prognostic state variable with source terms from water-column and sediment denitrification and a sink term from atmospheric degassing. The dN2dt rate function and the N2 reaeration coefficient kaN2_20 follow Zhang and Johnson (2016), Section 3.5. N2 is rarely used in practical applications but is exposed for completeness; if you do not need it, set use_N2: false to skip its integration.

    Algal Uptake¶

    Both floating and benthic algae draw nitrogen from the NH4 and NO3 pools in proportion to the ammonia preference factors PN (floating) and PNb (benthic):

    \[ \mathrm{NH4\_ApGrowth} = P_N \cdot r_{na}\,\mu_p\,A_p \]
    \[ \mathrm{NO3\_ApGrowth} = (1 - P_N)\,r_{na}\,\mu_p\,A_p \]

    Analogous expressions apply to benthic algae using PNb, rnb, the benthic growth rate, the fraction of benthic area Fb, and the depth normalization 1/h.

    Algal Respiration and Death¶

    Floating and benthic algae return nitrogen to the NH4 and OrgN pools during respiration and mortality:

    \[ \mathrm{NH4\_ApRespiration} = r_{na}\,k_{rp}\,A_p, \qquad \mathrm{ApDeath\_OrgN} = r_{na}\,k_{dp}\,A_p \]

    Benthic algal contributions include the fraction Fw that enters the water column on death and are normalized by depth.

    Couplings with Other State Variables¶

    • Floating algae (Ap): Algal growth withdraws NH4 and NO3; algal respiration returns NH4; algal death supplies OrgN.
    • Benthic algae (Ab): Same couplings as floating algae, scaled by Fb, Fw, and depth.
    • Dissolved oxygen (DOX): Nitrification consumes DOX through the stoichiometric ratio ron (see the Dissolved Oxygen page). Low DOX inhibits nitrification and activates denitrification.
    • Alkalinity (Alk): Nitrification decreases alkalinity through r_alkn; denitrification increases alkalinity through r_alkden; algal uptake of NH4 versus NO3 affects alkalinity through r_alkaa, r_alkan, r_alkba, and r_alkbn.

    Implementation Notes¶

    • Source: src/clearwater_modules/processes/nitrogen.py. The Nitrogen process class implements the OrgN, NH4, NO3, and N2 rate functions, with helpers for nitrification inhibition, bed release, water-column denitrification, and sediment denitrification.
    • Temperature-correction helpers apply the standard Arrhenius form rc20 * theta^(T - 20) from clearwater_modules.kinetics.arrhenius_correction.
    • Diagnostic totals exposed by the process include DIN = NH4 + NO3, TON = OrgN + rna * Ap, TKN = NH4 + TON, and TN = DIN + TON.

    Sediment fluxes are disabled by default

    The nitrogen defaults rnh4_20 = 0 and vno3_20 = 0 disable the sediment ammonium release and sediment denitrification fluxes. Set both to positive site-specific values when bed exchange is significant for your application.
