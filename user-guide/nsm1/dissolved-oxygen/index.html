    Dissolved Oxygen (DOX)¶

    Dissolved oxygen is the single most important water quality indicator in surface waters. DOX concentration reflects the net outcome of every biological, chemical, and physical process occurring in the water column and at the sediment-water interface. Phytoplankton and benthic algae add oxygen during photosynthesis and remove it during respiration. Nitrification, organic carbon oxidation, and carbonaceous biochemical oxygen demand (CBOD) each consume oxygen as microbial populations metabolize reduced species. Sediment oxygen demand (SOD) depletes oxygen at the bed. Atmospheric reaeration continuously drives the water-column concentration toward saturation.

    The classical dissolved oxygen sag curve in rivers below a waste load illustrates this balance: oxygen declines as oxidation reactions consume DOX faster than reaeration can replenish it, reaches a minimum where reaeration and demand match, and recovers downstream as demands decay. NSM1 resolves this balance explicitly by tracking nine rate terms that collectively determine the DOX budget.

    Role in Water Quality¶

    NSM1 treats DOX as the integrator of all oxygen-producing and oxygen-consuming processes in the water column. The model computes atmospheric reaeration from a user-selectable combination of hydraulic and wind-driven gas transfer formulations. The model computes biotic sources and sinks from the algal, benthic algal, nitrogen, carbon, and CBOD modules, so DOX is tightly coupled to every other NSM1 state variable. Low DOX inhibits nitrification, promotes denitrification, and triggers sediment nutrient release, so DOX also controls the behavior of the nitrogen cycle.

    flowchart LR
    Atm[Atmosphere] <-->|reaeration| DOX[DOX]
    Ap[Floating algae] -->|photosynthesis| DOX
    DOX -->|respiration| Ap
    Ab[Benthic algae] -->|photosynthesis| DOX
    DOX -->|respiration| Ab
    DOX -->|nitrification| NH4
    DOX -->|CBOD oxidation| CBOD
    DOX -->|DOC oxidation| DOC
    DOX -->|SOD| Sed[Sediment]

    Governing Equation¶

    The DOX mass balance, implemented in dDOXdt at processes.py ~line 3095, sums nine rate terms:

    \[ \frac{d[DOX]}{dt} = F_{rea} + F_{ApG} - F_{ApR} - F_{nit} - F_{DOCox} - F_{CBODox} + F_{AbG} - F_{AbR} - F_{SOD} \]

    Where each flux term has units of mg-O2 L-1 d-1:

    Symbol Term Source function
    \(F_{rea}\) Atmospheric reaeration Atm_O2_reaeration
    \(F_{ApG}\) Floating algae photosynthesis DOX_ApGrowth
    \(F_{ApR}\) Floating algae respiration DOX_ApRespiration
    \(F_{nit}\) Nitrification oxygen demand DOX_Nitrification
    \(F_{DOCox}\) DOC oxidation oxygen demand DOX_DOC_oxidation
    \(F_{CBODox}\) CBOD oxidation oxygen demand DOX_CBOD_oxidation
    \(F_{AbG}\) Benthic algae photosynthesis DOX_AbGrowth
    \(F_{AbR}\) Benthic algae respiration DOX_AbRespiration
    \(F_{SOD}\) Sediment oxygen demand DOX_SOD

    Source and Sink Terms¶

    Atmospheric reaeration¶

    NSM1 computes atmospheric oxygen exchange as a first-order relaxation toward saturation:

    \[ F_{rea} = k_a(T) \, (DOX_{sat} - DOX) \]

    The model computes the combined reaeration rate \(k_a(T)\) from a hydraulic component and a wind-driven component:

    \[ k_a(T) = \frac{k_{aw}(T)}{h} + k_{ah}(T) \]

    Here \(k_{ah}\) is a first-order hydraulic reaeration rate (1/d), \(k_{aw}\) is a wind-driven gas transfer velocity (m/d), and \(h\) is water depth.

    NSM1 offers nine hydraulic reaeration formulas selected by hydraulic_reaeration_option and thirteen wind reaeration formulas selected by wind_reaeration_option. Option 1 in each case uses the user-supplied values kah_20_user and kaw_20_user directly. The other options implement published correlations of O'Connor-Dobbins, Churchill, Owens-Gibbs, Tsivoglou, Thackston-Krenkel, Banks-Herrera, Wanninkhof, and others as functions of velocity, depth, flow, slope, shear velocity, and wind speed at 10 m. The subroutines kah_20 and kaw_20 at processes.py ~lines 50 and 125 enumerate the full set. Both components are corrected to ambient temperature with Arrhenius coefficients kah_theta and kaw_theta (default 1.024).

    Floating algae photosynthesis and respiration¶

    Photosynthesis by floating algae releases oxygen in proportion to the carbon fixed, with a stoichiometric bonus when ammonium rather than nitrate supplies cell nitrogen. The model computes:

    \[ F_{ApG} = ApGrowth \cdot r_{ca} \cdot r_{oc} \cdot \left( \frac{138}{106} - \frac{32}{106} f_{NH4} \right) \]

    Here \(r_{ca}\) is the algal carbon-to-chlorophyll ratio, \(r_{oc}\) is the oxygen-to-carbon stoichiometry (default ron = 2.0*32.0/14.0 for nitrogen, roc = 32/12 for carbon), and \(f_{NH4}\) is the fraction of algal N uptake drawn from NH4. Respiration consumes oxygen in direct proportion to biomass respired:

    \[ F_{ApR} = ApResp \cdot r_{ca} \cdot r_{oc} \]

    Benthic algae photosynthesis and respiration¶

    Benthic algae exchange oxygen with the overlying water column, scaled by the benthic coverage fraction Fb and divided by depth to convert areal fluxes to volumetric rates:

    \[ F_{AbG} = \left( \frac{138}{106} - \frac{32}{106} f^b_{NH4} \right) r_{oc} \, r_{cb} \, AbGrowth \, \frac{F_b}{h} \]
    \[ F_{AbR} = r_{oc} \, r_{cb} \, AbResp \, \frac{F_b}{h} \]

    Nitrification oxygen demand¶

    Nitrification oxidizes NH4 to NO3 and consumes oxygen at the stoichiometric ratio ron (2 times 32/14 = 4.57 mg-O2 per mg-N). The model suppresses the rate at low oxygen using an exponential inhibition factor controlled by KNR:

    \[ F_{nit} = \left( 1 - e^{-KNR \cdot DOX} \right) r_{on} \, k_{nit}(T) \, NH4 \]

    CBOD oxidation¶

    CBOD oxidation already carries units of oxygen demand, so the DOX flux is simply the CBOD oxidation rate summed across all CBOD groups:

    \[ F_{CBODox} = \sum_i CBOD_{ox,i} \]

    Dissolved organic carbon oxidation¶

    Heterotrophic oxidation of DOC consumes oxygen in proportion to carbon respired:

    \[ F_{DOCox} = r_{oc} \cdot DOC\_DIC\_oxidation \]

    Sediment oxygen demand¶

    SOD acts as an areal sink on the overlying water column. The model corrects the 20 deg C rate for temperature with an Arrhenius factor and for low oxygen with a Monod half-saturation:

    \[ F_{SOD} = \frac{1}{h} \cdot SOD_{20} \, \theta_{SOD}^{T-20} \cdot \frac{DOX}{DOX + K_{sSOD}} \]

    The subroutine SOD_tc at processes.py ~line 216 implements this correction.

    Dissolved Oxygen Saturation¶

    NSM1 computes DOX saturation from water temperature and atmospheric pressure using the APHA / Benson and Krause formulation. The uncorrected saturation concentration in fresh water at 1 atm is:

    \[ \ln DOX_{sat}^0 = -139.34410 + \frac{1.5757 \times 10^5}{T_K} - \frac{6.6423 \times 10^7}{T_K^2} + \frac{1.2438 \times 10^{10}}{T_K^3} - \frac{8.6219 \times 10^{11}}{T_K^4} \]

    The model corrects this baseline for ambient pressure and water vapor:

    \[ DOX_{sat} = DOX_{sat}^0 \cdot p_{atm} \cdot \frac{(1 - p_{wv}/p_{atm})(1 - \alpha \, p_{atm})}{(1 - p_{wv})(1 - \alpha)} \]

    Here \(p_{wv}\) is the partial pressure of water vapor computed by pwv at ~line 2878, and \(\alpha\) is a temperature-dependent correction coefficient computed by DOs_atm_alpha at ~line 2890. The routine DOX_sat at ~line 2901 performs the full evaluation.

    Parameters¶

    Parameter Units Scope Description
    ron mg-O2 / mg-N global DOX Oxygen-to-nitrogen stoichiometric ratio for nitrification (default 2.0 * 32.0 / 14.0)
    KsSOD mg-O2 L-1 global DOX Half-saturation for SOD oxygen dependence (default 1.0)
    SOD_20 mg-O2 m-2 d-1 global Sediment oxygen demand at 20 deg C (sentinel default 999; must be overridden)
    SOD_theta - global Arrhenius coefficient for SOD (sentinel default 999; must be overridden)
    kah_20_user 1/d global User-supplied hydraulic reaeration rate at 20 deg C, used when hydraulic_reaeration_option = 1 (sentinel default 999)
    kaw_20_user m/d global User-supplied wind reaeration velocity at 20 deg C, used when wind_reaeration_option = 1 (sentinel default 999)
    kah_theta - global Arrhenius coefficient for hydraulic reaeration (default 1.024)
    kaw_theta - global Arrhenius coefficient for wind reaeration (default 1.024)
    hydraulic_reaeration_option integer global Formula selector, 1--9 (default 1)
    wind_reaeration_option integer global Formula selector, 1--13 (default 1)
    KNR mg-O2 L-1 nitrogen Nitrification oxygen inhibition parameter (default 0.6)
    pressure_mb mb global Atmospheric pressure used in saturation calculation (default 2026.5, physically unrealistic; sea level is 1013.25)
    { .parameter-table }

    Default parameter sentinel values

    Several DOX-related parameters in DEFAULT_GLOBALVARS use the placeholder sentinel value 999, including SOD_20, SOD_theta, vsop, vs, kaw_20_user, and kah_20_user. These sentinels signal that users must supply site-specific values in the model configuration. Running NSM1 with the defaults as shipped will produce nonphysical results. In particular, SOD_theta = 999 combined with the Arrhenius correction \(\theta^{T-20}\) causes catastrophic exponential blow-up above 20 deg C: at 21 deg C the correction factor is 999, at 22 deg C it is ~10^6, and at 25 deg C it exceeds 10^15. The default pressure_mb = 2026.5 is also not a physical sea-level pressure. Input validation at model initialization, planned for v2, will reject or warn on sentinel values. Until then, always override these parameters in your configuration.

    Couplings with Other State Variables¶

    DOX is coupled to nearly every other NSM1 state variable:

    • Floating algae (Ap): photosynthesis produces DOX, respiration consumes DOX.
    • Benthic algae (Ab): photosynthesis and respiration exchange DOX across the bed, scaled by Fb/h.
    • Ammonia (NH4): nitrification consumes DOX in proportion to ron; the nitrification rate itself is DOX-limited via KNR.
    • Nitrate (NO3): low DOX promotes denitrification, which does not directly consume water-column DOX but shifts the nitrogen balance.
    • Dissolved organic carbon (DOC): heterotrophic oxidation consumes DOX at the ratio roc.
    • CBOD: first-order oxidation consumes DOX directly.
    • Alkalinity (Alk): nitrification and denitrification rates (which depend on DOX) drive alkalinity changes.
    • Sediment: SOD draws DOX from the overlying water column, attenuated at low oxygen by KsSOD.
    • Atmosphere: reaeration exchanges DOX across the air-water interface whenever DOX differs from saturation.

    Configuration¶

    Because DOX depends on many upstream variables and options, a typical configuration must supply reaeration, SOD, and saturation inputs consistently. The following YAML template illustrates the required entries. The v2 port of DOX is pending, so this template reflects v1 usage.

    global_parameters:
  use_DOX: true

global_vars:
  SOD_20: 0.5            # mg-O2/m2/d  (override sentinel)
  SOD_theta: 1.047       # (override sentinel)
  kah_20_user: 1.0       # 1/d         (override sentinel; used when hydraulic_reaeration_option = 1)
  kaw_20_user: 0.0       # m/d         (override sentinel; used when wind_reaeration_option = 1)
  kah_theta: 1.024
  kaw_theta: 1.024
  hydraulic_reaeration_option: 3
  wind_reaeration_option: 2
  pressure_mb: 1013.25   # sea level (override default 2026.5)

dox_parameters:
  ron: 4.571             # = 2 * 32 / 14
  KsSOD: 1.0

    Selecting a reaeration formulation

    The nine hydraulic options and thirteen wind options differ in their calibration ranges (shallow streams vs. deep rivers, low vs. high wind). Review the definitions in kah_20 and kaw_20 in processes.py and consult the Zhang and Johnson (2016) manual before selecting an option for your application.

    Implementation Notes¶

    • NSM1 source: src/clearwater_modules/nsm1/processes.py, function dDOXdt at ~line 3095. The function sums nine rate terms computed in the adjacent helpers (Atm_O2_reaeration, DOX_ApGrowth, DOX_ApRespiration, DOX_Nitrification, DOX_DOC_oxidation, DOX_CBOD_oxidation, DOX_AbGrowth, DOX_AbRespiration, DOX_SOD). Supporting routines DOX_sat, pwv, DOs_atm_alpha, ka_tc, kah_20, kah_tc, kaw_20, kaw_tc, and SOD_tc are defined earlier in the same file.
    • v2 implementation: the DOX process is not yet available in the v2 process-based architecture. See the developer guide's "Adding a Process" page for the structural pattern new processes follow.
    • Default constants: DEFAULT_DOX in src/clearwater_modules/nsm1/constants.py defines ron and KsSOD. Reaeration, SOD, and pressure parameters live in DEFAULT_GLOBALVARS and include the sentinel values discussed above.
    • Known issue: see docs/NSM1_DOX_rate_bug_investigation.md in the ClearWater-modules repository for the analysis of the SOD_theta = 999 blow-up and the recommended fix (input validation at model init rather than replacing sentinels with typical values).

    Stoichiometric consistency

    The DOX_ApGrowth implementation uses the stoichiometric expression (138/106 - 32 * ApUptakeFr_NH4 / 106), consistent with the Redfield-based derivation in Zhang and Johnson (2016). Verify that the algal and benthic algal C:Chl ratios (rca, rcb) and ammonium uptake preferences (ApUptakeFr_NH4, AbUptakeFr_NH4) are consistent between DOX, nitrogen, carbon, and alkalinity configurations.
